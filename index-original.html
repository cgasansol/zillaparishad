About Paschim Bardhaman

Paschim Bardhaman is a vibrant and culturally rich district in West Bengal, renowned as the industrial heartland of the state. Formed on 7th April 2017 after the bifurcation of the erstwhile Bardhaman district, it perfectly balances robust urban industrial zones like Asansol and Durgapur with serene natural beauty.

The Zilla Parishad is dedicated to enhancing the socio-economic framework, promoting rural infrastructure, and preserving the heritage of the region. Through this portal, we aim to provide accessible, comfortable, and secure tourism accommodations for everyone visiting the Land of Black Diamond.

State Government Schemes

Explore major welfare initiatives implemented by the Government of West Bengal for the citizens of Paschim Bardhaman.

Lakshmir Bhandar

Financial assistance program to provide basic income support to the female heads of families.

Swasthya Sathi

A basic health cover for secondary and tertiary care up to Rs. 5 Lakh per annum per family.

Kanyashree

An initiative to improve the life and status of girls by helping them pursue higher education.

Khadya Sathi

Ensuring food security by providing subsidized food grains to almost 90% of the state's population.

Explore Paschim Bardhaman

Discover the magnificent heritage, holy shrines, historic riverfronts, and eco-tourism marvels of our vibrant district. Click on any card to view detailed history and maps!

Holy Shrine

Ghagarburi Mandir

Bypass, Asansol

One of the oldest and most revered shrines dedicated to Goddess Chandi Mata near the Nunia river.

Open All Year
Spiritual Heritage

Maa Preth Kali (Kalipahari)

Kalipahari, Asansol

An iconic and unique temple located near Kalipahari, known for its deep-rooted local history and rituals.

Open Daily
Scenic Dam

Maithon Dam

Border of Paschim Bardhaman

Famous for its expansive blue reservoir, boating experiences, and South East Asia's first underground power station.

Best: July to Feb
Riverfront

Durgapur Barrage

Damodar River, Durgapur

Magnificent structure built across the mighty Damodar River, offering spectacular golden-hour sunsets.

Best: Monsoons & Winter
Ancient Temple

Kalyaneshwari Temple

Near Maithon, Asansol

A legendary 500-year-old temple dedicated to Goddess Kalyaneshwari, drawing thousands of devotees.

Open All Year
Eco-Tourism

Garh Jungle

Kanksa, Durgapur

An ancient forest steeped in mythology, historically linked with King Surath and Durga Puja.

Best: Winter Season
Architecture

Ichhai Ghosher Deul

Gourangapur, Ajay River

A remarkable architectural marvel—a towering brick rekha-deul temple set against the Ajay river.

Best: Oct to March
Cultural Hub

Churulia (Nazrul Gram)

Asansol Subdivision

The sacred birthplace of the national rebel poet Kazi Nazrul Islam, housing a dedicated museum.

Best: May
