Explore Paschim Bardhaman

Discover the magnificent heritage, holy shrines, historic riverfronts, and eco-tourism marvels of our vibrant district.

Holy Shrine

Ghagarburi Mandir

Bypass, Asansol

One of the oldest and most revered shrines dedicated to Goddess Chandi Mata near the Nunia river. A massive annual fair is hosted every January.

Open All Year
Spiritual Heritage

Maa Preth Kali (Kalipahari)

Kalipahari, Asansol

An iconic and unique temple located near Kalipahari, known for its deep-rooted local history, intense spiritual traditions, and serene atmosphere.

Open Daily
Scenic Dam

Maithon Dam

Border of Paschim Bardhaman

Famous for its expansive blue reservoir, boating experiences, and South East Asia's first underground hydroelectric power station.

Best: July to Feb
Riverfront

Durgapur Barrage

Damodar River, Durgapur

Magnificent structure built across the Damodar River, offering spectacular golden-hour sunsets and a popular local evening hangout.

Best: Monsoons & Winter
Ancient Temple

Kalyaneshwari Temple

Near Maithon, Asansol

A legendary 500-year-old temple dedicated to Goddess Kalyaneshwari, drawing thousands of devotees seeking blessings and peace.

Open All Year
Eco-Tourism

Garh Jungle

Kanksa, Durgapur

An ancient forest steeped in mythology, historically linked with King Surath and early roots of the legendary Durga Puja.

Best: Winter Season
Architecture

Ichhai Ghosher Deul

Gourangapur, Ajay River

A remarkable architectural marvel—a towering brick rekha-deul temple set against the picturesque backdrop of the Ajay river.

Best: Oct to March
Cultural Hub

Churulia (Nazrul Gram)

Asansol Subdivision

The sacred birthplace of the national rebel poet Kazi Nazrul Islam, housing a dedicated museum, library, and cultural center.

Best: May (Nazrul Jayanti)
