About Paschim Bardhaman

Paschim Bardhaman is a vibrant and culturally rich district in West Bengal, renowned as the industrial heartland of the state. Formed on 7th April 2017 after the bifurcation of the erstwhile Bardhaman district, it perfectly balances robust urban industrial zones like Asansol and Durgapur with serene natural beauty.

The Zilla Parishad is dedicated to enhancing the socio-economic framework, promoting rural infrastructure, and preserving the heritage of the region. Through this portal, we aim to provide accessible, comfortable, and secure tourism accommodations for everyone visiting the Land of Black Diamond.

Explore Paschim Bardhaman

Discover the magnificent heritage, holy shrines, historic riverfronts, and eco-tourism marvels of our vibrant district.

Holy Shrine

Ghagarburi Mandir

Bypass, Asansol

One of the oldest and most revered shrines dedicated to Goddess Chandi Mata near the Nunia river. A massive annual fair is hosted every January.

Open All Year
Spiritual Heritage

Maa Preth Kali (Kalipahari)

Kalipahari, Asansol

An iconic and unique temple located near Kalipahari, known for its deep-rooted local history, intense spiritual traditions, and serene atmosphere.

Open Daily
Scenic Dam

Maithon Dam

Border of Paschim Bardhaman

Famous for its expansive blue reservoir, boating experiences, and South East Asia's first underground hydroelectric power station.

Best: July to Feb
Riverfront

Durgapur Barrage

Damodar River, Durgapur

Magnificent structure built across the Damodar River, offering spectacular golden-hour sunsets and a popular local evening hangout.

Best: Monsoons & Winter
Ancient Temple

Kalyaneshwari Temple

Near Maithon, Asansol

A legendary 500-year-old temple dedicated to Goddess Kalyaneshwari, drawing thousands of devotees seeking blessings and peace.

Open All Year
Eco-Tourism

Garh Jungle

Kanksa, Durgapur

An ancient forest steeped in mythology, historically linked with King Surath and early roots of the legendary Durga Puja.

Best: Winter Season
Architecture

Ichhai Ghosher Deul

Gourangapur, Ajay River

A remarkable architectural marvel—a towering brick rekha-deul temple set against the picturesque backdrop of the Ajay river.

Best: Oct to March
Cultural Hub

Churulia (Nazrul Gram)

Asansol Subdivision

The sacred birthplace of the national rebel poet Kazi Nazrul Islam, housing a dedicated museum, library, and cultural center.

Best: May (Nazrul Jayanti)

Trip Planner (2 Days in Paschim Bardhaman)

Day 1: The Asansol Heritage Tour

Morning: Start your day with blessings at the ancient Ghagarburi Mandir.

Afternoon: Visit Churulia, the birthplace of rebel poet Kazi Nazrul Islam.

Evening: Check-in and relax at Bardhaman Bhaban ZP Guest House.

Day 2: Durgapur Nature & Dams

Morning: Drive to Maithon Dam for boating in the blue reservoir.

Afternoon: Explore the eco-tourism spot at Garh Jungle.

Evening: Witness a breathtaking sunset at the Durgapur Barrage.
